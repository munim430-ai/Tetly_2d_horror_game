About the Game

You wake on a cold metal table with no memory of how you got there. Ward 13 of Blackridge Psychiatric Institute surrounds you—abandoned, dark, and something is hunting you by sound alone.

Armed with only a dwindling flashlight and your wits, navigate through three locked zones to find the keys to escape. But beware: The Patient is listening. Every heartbeat, every breath, every footfall brings it closer.

Core Mechanics

  • Flashlight System: Battery drains in real time. Darkness makes you vulnerable.
  • Fear Meter: Darkness and The Patient's presence elevate your fear. High fear causes erratic movement.
  • Stealth & Hiding: Use lockers to hide and silence your presence.
  • Three Keys: Scattered across ground floor, basement, and third floor. Collect them to escape.

Story

The Patient

Former occupant of Ward 13. Diagnosed records are redacted. Now the entity hunts through absolute darkness, guided only by sound. What was it? What did it become?

The Institution

Blackridge Psychiatric Institute was sealed after Week 7. All staff evacuated. Files marked CLASSIFIED. The building remains, waiting for the curious... or the desperate.

Your Escape

Three keys. Three locked zones. One path out. But The Patient is listening. It can hear your heartbeat from two rooms away. Can you escape before the darkness consumes you?
